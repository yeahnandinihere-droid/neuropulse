# NEUROPULSE
### See the Signal. Unlock Better Care.

**NEUROPULSE** is the first product under **BIOPTIQ — The Future of Vision.**

NEUROPULSE is an experimental pupil-response research platform designed to support the organization, visualization and computational exploration of pupil-response recordings.

## About BIOPTIQ

BIOPTIQ is a vision-technology initiative focused on exploring digital approaches to physiological data and healthcare technology.

## About NEUROPULSE

NEUROPULSE aims to provide a structured digital environment for pupil-response recording and research.

The platform's development direction includes:

- Pupil-response video acquisition
- Recording inspection and visualization
- Pupil detection and tracking
- Quantitative pupil-response analysis
- Longitudinal recording organization
- Physiological research exploration

## Current Platform Features

- Video upload and preview
- Recording metadata inspection
- Video frame extraction
- Recording session organization
- Basic user profile
- Research workspace

## Technology Stack

- Python
- Streamlit
- OpenCV
- NumPy
- Pandas
- Plotly
- SciPy
- Pillow

## Development Vision

NEUROPULSE is being developed to explore computational approaches to pupil-response recordings, with future work focused on image processing, quantitative analysis, reproducibility and research validation.

## Research Disclaimer

NEUROPULSE is an experimental research project. It is not a validated medical device and is not intended for clinical diagnosis, screening or treatment decisions.
